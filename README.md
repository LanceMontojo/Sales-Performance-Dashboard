# Sales-Performance-Dashboard

The first page gives a clear snapshot of how the business is doing overall. It begins with the key performance indicators: total sales, transactions, products sold, and average order value. Year‑over‑year comparisons make it easy to spot whether things are improving or slipping.

Supporting visuals then expand on these results. The sales trend chart illustrates changes in performance across months. Product and regional views highlight the strongest contributors to sales. The payment method breakdown adds another layer, showing how customers are choosing to pay and how revenue is spread across different channels. 

The purpose of this page is to establish context before moving into more detailed analysis. Rather than focusing on individual records, it emphasizes overall trends and major contributors.

## Top Performance by Region 

The second page takes a closer look at how each region is performing. It brings together key information such as total sales, the region manager, top salesperson, and star product, while the supporting visuals show the impact of promotions and how the selected product performs in terms of sales, units sold, return rate, average price, and delivery time. The Product Explorer lets users go one step further by selecting an individual product and viewing its related metrics and sales trend.

When the page is first opened, it shows the overall performance of the selected default region and highlights its leading product. The promotion breakdown shows which promotions contribute the most to sales, while the selected product is summarized through key metrics such as sales, units sold, return rate, average price, and delivery time.

Filtering the page to East in 2024 shows Eric as the region manager, Eva as the Sales MVP, and Printer as the star product, making it one of the main contributors to the region’s performance. Looking at Printer more closely, its key metrics provide a better picture of its performance through sales, products sold, return rate, average price, and delivery time. Despite being the top product, its sales were not consistent throughout the year, with no sales recorded in March, September, and October, while August had the highest sales. This shows that being the leading product overall does not necessarily mean it performed consistently every month.
